The Know Your Holes Discovery Class

WholeClass.app is a personal development and educational platform designed to provide a safe, supportive and trusted environment where individuals can discover, understand and address their personal holes—unmet needs that create blind spots and behavioral patterns that may limit their growth.

Inspired by the book Know Your Holes, WholeClass guides members through structured self-discovery, personal assessments, educational exercises, reflection and accountability. Members can participate individually or through guided discovery classes.

The platform promotes personal ownership without judgment, encouraging members to recognize their strengths, understand their limitations and develop actionable strategies for meaningful improvement.

Our Mission

To empower individuals through self-awareness, education and intentional personal development.

Our Vision

To build a trusted community where discovering your holes becomes an opportunity for growth rather than a reason for shame.

Our Core Values
  • Trust and confidentiality
  • Self-awareness
  • Personal accountability
  • Respect and acceptance
  • Continuous personal growth

The 14-Day Discovery Class

Everyone carries holes. Most of us have never named ours.

A hole is an unmet need. Left unmet, it creates blind spots and behavioral patterns that start making decisions for you. The book names 25 of them. Over 14 days you'll look at every one, surface first and core last, and finish knowing which five matter most.

Before you start

Trust and confidentiality come first.

Private by default. Your scores, Hunts and letters are never sold and never shown to anyone you haven't chosen.
Groups see completion only. If you join through a guided group, your facilitator sees which days you finished. Never your answers.
Export or delete any time.
Education, not treatment. WholeClass helps you understand your needs. It doesn't diagnose, and it doesn't replace a counselor.

Before the Core Holes

A note of care

The next four days go to the oxygen holes: Trust, Emotional Safety, Respect, Communication, Security, Loyalty and Physical Intimacy. These often reach back further than any relationship you're in now.

Go slowly. Skip a question if you need to. If something surfaces that feels too heavy to carry alone, such as past abuse or trauma, stop and talk it through with a licensed counselor, your pastor, or someone you trust.

If you're ever thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (U.S.) any time.
