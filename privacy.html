Privacy Policy

WholeClass™ · The Know Your Holes Discovery Class

Effective date: November 25, 2026

WholeClass is built on trust and confidentiality. This policy explains what Merch Corporation ("we," "us") collects through WholeClass and wholeclass.app (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information you give us

  • Account details: your first and last name, email address, password (stored only in encrypted form), your faith setting, and your confirmation that you are 18 or older.
  • Your class work: your Quick Five and re-scores, answers to the IWK questions, Hunts, Daily Diagnosis Boxes, actions and takeaways, your Hole Map, and your IWK Letter.
  • Access and purchases: book keys, group codes and gifted seats you use or give, and a record of your purchases (amount, date and status). Card payments are handled by Stripe; we do not receive your full card number.
  • Messages to us when you contact support.

2. Information collected automatically

Basic technical information needed to run the Service, such as sign-in sessions, device and browser type, and error logs kept by our hosting providers. The app stores a sign-in token and cached app files on your device so it can open quickly. We do not use advertising trackers.

3. Sensitive information

What you write about your relationships, needs and experiences is personal. You choose what to write. We use it only to show it back to you and to calculate your rankings. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never show it to anyone else.

4. Who can see your information

  • You can see all of your own information.
  • Guided group facilitators can see your name, the days you have finished and whether you completed the class. They cannot see your answers, scores or letters.
  • Someone who gave you a seat can see that the seat was accepted. They cannot see your answers.
  • No other member can see your information.

5. How we use information

To provide the class and save your progress, process payments and access, run guided groups, send account emails (such as confirming your account or resetting your password), tell you about new courses if you ask us to, provide support, keep the Service secure, and meet legal obligations.

6. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the Service, such as our database and hosting providers (Supabase, which stores data in the United States, and GitHub Pages) and our payment processor (Stripe), under their privacy and security terms; when the law requires it; to protect the safety and rights of people or the Service; or as part of a merger or sale of the business, subject to this policy.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your account and class work while your account is open. If you erase your answers in the app, they are deleted from our database (backups roll off within a limited time). Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require.

8. Your choices

  • Export everything you have saved from More › My data.
  • Erase your answers and start over from More.
  • Change your faith setting at any time.
  • Delete your account by emailing askpaul.pp@gmail.com from your account email.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have other rights to access, correct or delete your information. Contact us and we will respond as the law requires.

9. Adults only

The Service is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use encryption in transit, row-level security so each member can reach only their own records, and limited administrative access. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy by posting a new version with a new effective date and, for material changes, notice in the app or by email.
